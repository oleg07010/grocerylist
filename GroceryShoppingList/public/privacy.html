GrocerySync — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

GrocerySync is a simple shared grocery-list app. This policy explains what data the app handles and how. It applies to the GrocerySync web app and the GrocerySync apps for Android and iOS, which all use the same shared list.

Signing in

GrocerySync asks you to sign in with your Google account. Sign-in is handled by Google (Google Sign-In and Firebase Authentication). When you sign in, Firebase Authentication keeps an account for you with:

  • Your Google account's email address
  • The basic profile Google shares at sign-in: your name and a link to your profile photo
  • An account ID, and when the account was created and last signed in

Your email address decides whether you can open the list: only the Google accounts on the household's list can see or change it. Signing in with any other Google account creates the same sign-in record but gives no access to the list. GrocerySync never sees your Google password.

What the app stores

Besides your sign-in account, the only data GrocerySync stores is the content you type into your list:

  • Grocery item names and optional details (brand, size, notes)
  • Section names, and per-item flags (checked off, pinned, ordering)

This content is saved to Google Firebase Firestore, a cloud database, so the list stays in sync across devices. List items are not tied to the account that added them. Everything is transmitted over an encrypted (HTTPS/TLS) connection.

What the app does not collect

  • No phone numbers
  • No advertising, and no ad identifiers
  • No analytics, tracking, or third-party trackers
  • No location, contacts, photos from your device, or device identifiers

Your data is not sold or shared with third parties. Google (Firebase) acts only as the service provider that stores it on our behalf.

The list is shared. GrocerySync is designed for a household to share a single list. Everyone using the same app/list can see, add, edit, and remove the same items. Please don't enter anything private or sensitive.

Deleting your account and data

Your sign-in account is kept until you ask us to delete it. To delete your GrocerySync account, email oleg.appassov@gmail.com from the Google account you sign in with (or tell us which address it is). We will delete your sign-in account (email address, name, photo link and account ID) and remove your email from the household's list.

Grocery items are shared by the household and not tied to an account, so deleting your account does not remove them. Items remain in the list until they are deleted: you can delete any item at any time from within the app (each item's ··· menu → Delete), or ask us in the same email to remove stored list data.

Children

GrocerySync is a general-audience utility and is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will change. Continued use of the app after an update means you accept the revised policy.
