## Full description (≤4000 chars)
```
GrocerySync is a clean, fast, shared grocery list for your household. Add an
item on your phone and it appears instantly on everyone else's — no refresh,
no fuss. One list, always in sync.

WHY GROCERYSYNC
• Real-time sync — changes show up instantly for everyone sharing the list.
• Beautifully simple — a calm, uncluttered design that's quick to use in the aisle.
• No account needed — open it and start adding. Nothing to sign up for.
• Free, and no ads.

FEATURES
• Add items with optional details (brand, size, notes).
• Check items off as you shop — the progress bar shows what's left.
• Organize items into sections (Produce, Dairy, your favourite store…).
• Pin the staples you always buy so they survive a reset.
• Reset for a new week with one tap — checked items are unchecked, nothing is lost.
• Search to filter a long list in a second.

MADE FOR SHARING
GrocerySync is designed for a couple or a family to share a single list. Everyone
sees the same items and the same checkmarks, live. Perfect for "we're out of
milk" moments.

Your list data is stored securely and used only to power the app — no ads, no
tracking, no selling your data. See our privacy policy for details.
```
